Business travel is often about making every journey predictable and every day efficient.

The best accommodation is not always the closest to your destination. Reliable transport, flexible connections and a comfortable place to work or relax after meetings usually have a greater impact on a successful business trip.

## Choosing the right area

Different business trips require different priorities.

### If most meetings are in central Milan

Choose accommodation close to a metro station rather than focusing only on a central postcode. Reliable public transport often makes moving between appointments quicker and more predictable.

### If you're attending exhibitions

Staying somewhere with a direct connection to **Rho Fiera Milano** is often more practical than booking next to the exhibition centre itself.

Our guide to [Where to Stay Near Rho Fiera Milano](/guides/where-to-stay-near-rho-fiera-milano) explains the best accommodation strategy.

### If your schedule includes several locations

Business travellers visiting clients across different parts of Milan usually benefit from neighbourhoods with straightforward metro and railway connections.

Professionals working near the university district may also find our guide to [Where to Stay Near Bicocca University](/guides/where-to-stay-near-bicocca-university) useful.

### If you're staying for several days

Residential neighbourhoods often provide a better balance of comfort, convenience and value than the busiest central districts, especially when you need space to work or unwind after meetings.

For a practical overview of moving between meetings, stations and exhibition venues, read our guide to [How to Use Milan Public Transport](/guides/how-to-use-milan-public-transport).

## Frequently asked questions

### Should I stay near my meeting location?

If most of your appointments are in one area, staying nearby can be convenient. If you'll be travelling across Milan, choosing accommodation with reliable public transport is often the better option.

### Is the city centre always the best choice for business travel?

Not necessarily. Well-connected residential neighbourhoods often provide a better balance of comfort, convenience and value, especially for longer stays.

### Is Milan's public transport reliable for business trips?

In most cases, yes. The metro network offers predictable journey times and makes it easy to move between many of the city's main business districts.

### What if I have meetings in different parts of Milan?

Choose accommodation with straightforward metro or railway connections rather than focusing on a single destination. This gives you greater flexibility throughout your stay.

### Are apartments a good option for business travellers?

For stays of several nights, many professionals appreciate having more space, privacy and a comfortable place to work between meetings.
