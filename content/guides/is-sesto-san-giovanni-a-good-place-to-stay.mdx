If you're wondering whether staying outside central Milan is a good idea, Sesto San Giovanni is one of the strongest options to consider.

With direct access to the **M1 Red Line**, competitive accommodation prices and convenient transport links, it offers many of the advantages visitors look for without the higher costs of the historic centre.

This guide helps you decide whether Sesto San Giovanni is the right base for your trip.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Sesto San Giovanni part of Milan?

No. It is an independent municipality immediately north of Milan, but it is fully connected to the city through the M1 metro, regional trains and the road network.

### How long does it take to reach central Milan?

The journey to **Duomo** takes around 20 minutes on the **M1 Red Line**, depending on your departure station and waiting time.

### Is Sesto San Giovanni a good base for tourists?

Yes. It combines direct public transport, better value and a quieter environment than many central neighbourhoods.

### Can I stay here without a car?

Absolutely. Excellent metro and rail connections make it an easy base for exploring Milan and nearby destinations.

For more information, read our guides to [Where to Stay in Milan Without a Car](/guides/where-to-stay-in-milan-without-a-car) and [How to Use Milan Public Transport](/guides/how-to-use-milan-public-transport).

### Is Sesto San Giovanni convenient for Rho Fiera Milano?

Yes. The **M1 Red Line** provides a direct connection, making the area a practical alternative for visitors attending exhibitions and conferences.

Read our guide to [Where to Stay Near Rho Fiera Milano](/guides/where-to-stay-near-rho-fiera-milano) for more detailed advice.

### Is it suitable for families?

Yes. Families often appreciate the quieter surroundings, spacious accommodation and convenient transport connections.
