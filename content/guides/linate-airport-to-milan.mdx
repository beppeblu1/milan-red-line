## Linate Airport at a glance

Linate is Milan's closest airport.

Unlike Malpensa and Orio al Serio:

- there is only one passenger terminal;
- the airport is directly connected to the M4 Metro;
- reaching central Milan usually requires no airport railway service or shuttle transfer.

Most visitors only need to decide between:

- M4 Metro;
- airport bus;
- official taxi or private transfer.

{/* rx:transport */}

## The main ways to reach Milan

### M4 Metro

The M4 Blue Line is usually the best option for visitors staying within Milan.

The station is directly connected to the airport terminal and provides a fast connection towards the city.

The line reaches **San Babila**, where you can continue towards many destinations using Milan's Metro network. Along the route, M4 also connects with M2 and M3, making transfers across the city straightforward.

The Metro is particularly useful if you want:

- a predictable journey;
- to avoid road traffic;
- easy onward Metro connections;
- a simple transfer into the city.

A standard Milan urban ticket is valid on the M4 because it is part of the ATM Metro network. For a practical overview of fares, validation and contactless travel, read [Milan Public Transport Tickets](/guides/milan-public-transport-tickets).

### Airport bus

Airport buses still connect Linate with Milano Centrale.

They may be useful if:

- Centrale is already your destination;
- you prefer remaining on one vehicle;
- your accommodation is close to the station.

Unlike the Metro, buses are affected by traffic conditions.

Before boarding, verify:

- destination;
- operator;
- departure bay;
- current timetable.

### Taxi or private transfer

An official taxi or private transfer offers door-to-door travel.

It can be a practical choice if you:

- travel as a group;
- have heavy luggage;
- arrive late;
- travel with children;
- prefer maximum convenience.

Always use the official taxi rank outside the terminal.

## Continue your journey from San Babila

For many visitors, San Babila is not the final destination but the point where the rest of Milan becomes easy to reach.

From San Babila you can:

- continue directly into the historic centre;
- change to the **M1 Red Line**;
- reach Duomo in just one stop;
- continue towards Sesto San Giovanni using M1.

If your accommodation is near the Red Line, changing at San Babila is often the most straightforward option.

For more detailed guidance, read [How to Use the Milan Metro](/guides/how-to-use-the-milan-metro) and [Where to Stay Near the M1 Red Line](/guides/where-to-stay-near-the-m1-red-line).

## Which option should you choose?

### Choose the M4 when...

The Metro is usually the best choice if you:

- are staying in Milan;
- want a predictable journey;
- plan to use the Metro network afterwards;
- are travelling during busy road traffic.

### Choose the airport bus when...

The bus may be useful if:

- Milano Centrale is your destination;
- the current departure is more convenient than the Metro;
- you prefer avoiding an interchange.

If you choose the bus, [How to Use Milano Centrale Station](/guides/milano-centrale-station-guide) explains how to find the Metro and continue from the station.

### Choose a taxi when...

A taxi is often worth considering if:

- several people are travelling together;
- you have heavy luggage;
- your destination requires multiple changes;
- convenience matters more than the lowest cost.

## Arriving late

Before leaving the airport, check:

- current Metro service;
- any planned engineering work;
- the availability of airport buses;
- official taxi availability.

Late arrivals may reduce the number of available public transport options.

Always verify live service information before travelling.

## Travelling with luggage, children or reduced mobility

### Families

The M4 generally provides the simplest journey because it avoids road traffic and unnecessary transfers.

Allow additional time for:

- baggage reclaim;
- lifts;
- ticket purchase;
- boarding.

### Heavy luggage

The Metro works well for ordinary luggage.

If your accommodation requires several changes after San Babila, compare the convenience of a taxi.

### Reduced mobility

The M4 stations are designed with lifts and escalators. Before travelling, check the current status of accessibility facilities if they are essential for your journey.

## Common mistakes

### Looking for an airport train

The M4 Metro already serves the airport directly.

There is no need for a separate airport rail service.

### Ignoring your final destination

Think about where you need to go after reaching San Babila.

Your onward Metro connection is often more important than the airport transfer itself.

### Choosing a taxi automatically

For many visitors, the Metro is faster and simpler than expected.

Compare the complete journey before deciding.

### Forgetting the onward journey after Centrale

If you travel by airport bus, remember that reaching Milano Centrale is usually only one part of the journey.

You may still need the Metro, a taxi or a final walk to reach your accommodation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does the Metro go directly to Linate Airport?

Yes. The M4 Blue Line serves the airport directly.

### Is the M4 the easiest way into Milan?

For most visitors, yes. It provides a predictable connection into the city and allows onward travel through the Metro network.

### Do I still need an airport shuttle?

Usually not. The Metro has replaced the need for an airport shuttle for many journeys into Milan.

### How do I reach the M1 from Linate?

Take M4 to San Babila and change to M1.

### Is a normal Metro ticket valid from Linate?

Yes. Linate is part of the Milan urban Metro network. See [Milan Public Transport Tickets](/guides/milan-public-transport-tickets) for the current ticketing principles.

### Should I take a taxi instead?

A taxi is most useful for groups, heavy luggage or destinations that would require several public transport changes.

### Can I reach Sesto San Giovanni by Metro?

Yes. Take M4 to San Babila, change to M1 and continue north towards the station nearest your final destination.

### What if I arrive after the Metro closes?

Check the current night replacement service, airport buses or the official taxi rank.
