## Why not stay next to the hospital?

People visiting San Raffaele Hospital often have different priorities from leisure travellers.

Medical appointments, scheduled admissions, follow-up visits and longer stays all benefit from accommodation that's comfortable, practical and easy to reach.

Because the hospital is well connected by public transport, many visitors choose accommodation outside the immediate area without making daily journeys significantly more complicated.

When comparing different options, think about your complete door-to-door journey, the services available nearby and how comfortable the neighbourhood will feel during your stay.

## Getting to San Raffaele Hospital

Most public transport journeys to the hospital pass through **Cascina Gobba** on the **M2 Green Line**, where visitors continue using the dedicated light metro or **ATM bus 925**.

Visitors staying in **Sesto San Giovanni** usually travel on the **M1 Red Line**, change to the **M2** and continue from Cascina Gobba to the hospital.

As with any medical appointment, it's worth allowing a little extra time and checking the latest transport information before travelling.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is it necessary to stay next to San Raffaele Hospital?

Not necessarily. Thanks to Milan's public transport network, many well-connected residential areas provide an easy journey to the hospital while offering a wider choice of accommodation.

### How easy is it to reach San Raffaele Hospital by public transport?

Most visitors travel via **Cascina Gobba** on the **M2 Green Line**, then continue using the dedicated light metro or **ATM bus 925**. The journey is straightforward from many parts of Milan.

### Is Sesto San Giovanni convenient for hospital visits?

For many visitors, yes. It combines reliable public transport, useful everyday services and a quieter residential environment, making it particularly suitable for longer stays.

### What facilities are useful during a longer stay?

Many visitors appreciate having supermarkets, pharmacies, cafés and restaurants within walking distance, together with accommodation that offers enough space to relax between appointments.

### Is an apartment a good choice?

For stays of several days or when accompanying a patient, an apartment often provides more space, greater flexibility and the convenience of facilities such as a kitchen and washing machine.
