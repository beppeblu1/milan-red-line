Getting from **Orio al Serio Airport to Milan** is usually easiest by direct airport coach, but that is not the only option.

You can also take a local airport bus to Bergamo railway station and continue by train, or choose an official taxi or private transfer for a door-to-door journey. The best option depends on your arrival time, luggage, group size and final destination in Milan.

## Who is this guide for?

This guide is useful if you are:

- arriving at Orio al Serio for the first time;
- unsure whether the airport is in Milan or Bergamo;
- comparing a direct coach with the train route via Bergamo;
- travelling towards Milano Centrale;
- continuing towards the Milan Metro or Sesto San Giovanni;
- arriving late;
- travelling with children or heavy luggage;
- considering a taxi or private transfer.

## Orio al Serio Airport to Milan at a glance

For most visitors, the practical choices are:

- **direct airport coach:** usually the simplest public transport option;
- **Airport Bus to Bergamo station, then train:** useful when the rail timetable or a stop along the route suits your plans;
- **official taxi or private transfer:** useful for groups, heavy luggage, late arrivals or door-to-door travel;
- **rental car:** generally unnecessary when Milan is your main destination.

The main decision is:

> Do you prefer a direct journey to Milano Centrale, or are you comfortable changing in Bergamo?

## First, understand the airport name

The same airport may appear under several names:

- **Milan Bergamo Airport**
- **Bergamo Airport**
- **Orio al Serio Airport**
- **BGY**

These names refer to the same airport.

The airport is near Bergamo rather than central Milan. This explains why the transfer is longer than the journey from Linate and why the rail option requires an intermediate connection.

Do not confuse:

- the airport terminal;
- Bergamo railway station;
- Milano Centrale.

They are three separate places.

### After baggage reclaim

Before choosing a departure, allow time to:

1. leave the aircraft;
2. complete passport control where required;
3. collect checked luggage;
4. enter the arrivals area;
5. locate the coach bays, local bus stop or taxi rank;
6. buy or activate the correct ticket.

Your flight’s scheduled landing time is not the time you will be ready to leave the airport.

## The main ways to reach Milan

### Direct airport coach to Milano Centrale

For many first-time visitors, the direct airport coach is the easiest option.

Coaches normally depart from the airport arrivals area and travel towards **Milano Centrale**.

This option is useful when:

- you want a direct public transport journey;
- Milano Centrale is convenient for your destination;
- you prefer to avoid changing vehicles in Bergamo;
- you are travelling with ordinary airline luggage;
- you plan to continue by Metro, train or taxi from Centrale.

Before boarding, verify:

- the operator;
- the destination;
- the departure bay;
- the current departure time;
- the ticket conditions.

Several vehicles may be waiting outside the terminal. Do not assume that every coach serves the same destination.

### What to expect from the coach

The main advantage is simplicity:

- board at the airport;
- store larger luggage as instructed;
- leave the coach near Milano Centrale;
- continue towards your final destination.

The main disadvantage is road traffic.

Journey times can vary, especially during:

- weekday commuting periods;
- motorway congestion;
- major events;
- poor weather;
- road incidents.

Compare the complete journey rather than only the scheduled coach duration.

### Airport Bus to Bergamo station, then train

The rail-based alternative requires two separate stages:

1. take the local **Airport Bus** from the terminal to Bergamo railway station;
2. take a regional train from Bergamo to Milan.

This option may be useful when:

- the train timetable works particularly well for you;
- you want to stop in Bergamo;
- you prefer rail travel for the longer part of the journey;
- traffic towards Milan is expected to be difficult;
- your onward plan already involves the railway network.

The local airport bus and the regional train are separate services. Check whether you need separate tickets and allow enough time for the change at Bergamo station.

### Is the train route faster?

Not necessarily.

Although the train from Bergamo to Milan is predictable, you must also include:

- waiting for the airport bus;
- travelling from the airport to Bergamo station;
- transferring between bus and train;
- waiting for the train;
- managing luggage during the change.

The direct coach often remains simpler for a visitor whose destination is Milan.

The bus-and-train route becomes more attractive when its timetable, reliability or connection pattern works better for your specific journey.

### Official taxi or private transfer

A taxi or private transfer provides a door-to-door journey.

It may be worth considering if you are:

- travelling in a group;
- carrying several large bags;
- travelling with young children;
- arriving when public transport is limited;
- reaching an address that requires several changes;
- travelling with reduced mobility;
- prioritising simplicity over price.

Use the official taxi rank outside arrivals or a transfer booked through a reliable provider.

Avoid accepting unsolicited transport offers inside or immediately outside the terminal.

### Rental car

A rental car is usually unnecessary when Milan is your main destination.

Urban traffic, parking and restricted-access zones can make driving less convenient than public transport.

A car may be useful when:

- Milan is not your final destination;
- you are continuing towards a rural area;
- your itinerary includes locations poorly served by rail;
- you need a vehicle for the rest of the trip.

## Direct coach or train via Bergamo?

This is the central decision for most visitors.

### Choose the direct coach when…

A direct coach is usually the better choice when:

- you want the fewest steps;
- Milano Centrale is a convenient arrival point;
- you have luggage;
- you do not want to coordinate two separate services;
- you are unfamiliar with the local transport system;
- traffic conditions appear reasonable.

### Choose the route via Bergamo station when…

The Airport Bus and train combination may be useful when:

- you want to visit Bergamo before Milan;
- a train departure aligns well with your arrival;
- rail travel is more convenient for your onward plan;
- you prefer avoiding the motorway for the main part of the journey;
- you are comfortable managing a transfer.

### Do not assume the train is direct from the terminal

Unlike Malpensa, the standard rail journey does not begin with an airport train that continues directly into Milan.

You must first reach Bergamo railway station.

This extra transfer is the most important practical difference between Orio al Serio and Malpensa.

## Arriving at Milano Centrale

Most direct airport coaches terminate in the Milano Centrale area.

After leaving the coach:

- confirm which side of the station you are on;
- follow signs if you need the main railway concourse;
- locate M2 or M3 if continuing by Metro;
- allow time for the walk between the coach stop and your next connection.

Milano Centrale is large and busy.

Our guide to [How to Use Milano Centrale Station](/guides/milano-centrale-station-guide) explains the station layout, Metro access and onward navigation.

## How to continue towards Sesto San Giovanni

From Milano Centrale, travellers heading towards Sesto San Giovanni normally need to enter the Metro network and connect to the **M1 Red Line**.

A common journey pattern is:

1. take **M2** from Centrale;
2. change to **M1** at an appropriate interchange;
3. continue north towards the station nearest your final destination.

The exact route depends on:

- your accommodation;
- current Metro service;
- luggage;
- lift availability;
- the time of arrival.

See [How to Use the Milan Metro](/guides/how-to-use-the-milan-metro) for directions, termini and interchanges.

You can also read [Where to Stay Near the M1 Red Line](/guides/where-to-stay-near-the-m1-red-line) to understand which areas provide the simplest connections.

## Which option should you choose?

### Choose the direct coach for simplicity

Usually consider the coach when you want:

- one vehicle from the airport to Milan;
- straightforward luggage handling;
- arrival near Milano Centrale;
- minimal decision-making after landing.

### Choose the train route for flexibility

Consider the route via Bergamo when:

- you want to stop in Bergamo;
- the train schedule suits you;
- you prefer rail for the longer stage;
- motorway disruption makes the coach less attractive.

### Choose a taxi for door-to-door travel

Consider an official taxi or private transfer when:

- several people can share the cost;
- your destination requires multiple changes;
- you have heavy luggage;
- you arrive late;
- accessibility and convenience are the priority.

## Arriving late or during disruption

Late arrivals require additional planning.

Before travelling, verify:

- the last suitable direct coach;
- the final Airport Bus to Bergamo;
- the last useful train from Bergamo;
- the operating hours of your onward Metro connection;
- current road or rail disruption.

Do not build your plan around the aircraft’s scheduled landing time.

Allow for:

- flight delays;
- passport control;
- baggage delivery;
- walking to the coach bays;
- ticket purchase;
- missed connections.

If public transport is no longer practical, the main alternatives are normally:

- an official taxi;
- a pre-booked transfer;
- waiting for the next available service where appropriate.

Always check current operational information before travelling.

## Travelling with children, luggage or reduced mobility

### Families

For families, the direct coach often reduces complexity because it avoids the transfer at Bergamo station.

Allow additional time for:

- toilets;
- pushchairs;
- luggage loading;
- ticket purchase;
- finding the correct departure bay.

### Heavy luggage

The coach may be easier when you have large suitcases because the train route requires:

- boarding a local bus;
- unloading at Bergamo station;
- reaching the correct platform;
- boarding the train;
- continuing again after arriving in Milan.

A taxi or transfer may become more attractive for a larger group with several bags.

### Reduced mobility

Accessibility should be checked for the complete route.

Verify:

- airport assistance arrangements;
- coach boarding conditions;
- accessibility at Bergamo station;
- lift availability;
- the accessibility of your onward Metro stations.

Do not assume that every transfer will be step-free.

## Common mistakes to avoid

### Assuming the airport is in central Milan

Orio al Serio is near Bergamo.

Plan for a significant onward journey before reaching Milan.

### Looking for a direct airport train

The usual train route requires a local bus to Bergamo railway station first.

Do not follow signs expecting a Malpensa-style rail connection.

### Boarding the first coach outside arrivals

Check:

- operator;
- destination;
- departure time;
- bay number.

Not every coach outside the terminal is travelling to Milano Centrale.

### Comparing only the train segment

The Bergamo–Milan train is only one part of the rail-based journey.

Include the Airport Bus and transfer time.

### Choosing a tight train connection

Airport arrival times are unpredictable.

Leave enough margin between landing and a planned departure from Bergamo station.

### Forgetting the onward journey from Centrale

Reaching Milano Centrale does not necessarily mean that you have reached your final destination.

Include the Metro, taxi or final walk in your planning.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Orio al Serio the same as Milan Bergamo Airport?

Yes. Orio al Serio Airport, Bergamo Airport, Milan Bergamo Airport and airport code BGY refer to the same airport.

### Is there a direct train from Orio al Serio Airport to Milan?

The usual rail journey requires taking the Airport Bus to Bergamo railway station and then a regional train to Milan. Check current airport information before travelling because transport infrastructure may evolve.

### Is the coach or train better from Bergamo Airport?

The direct coach is normally simpler because it avoids a transfer. The route via Bergamo station may be useful when you want to visit Bergamo, prefer rail travel or find a particularly convenient connection.

### Where do airport coaches arrive in Milan?

The main direct airport coaches generally serve the Milano Centrale area. Always confirm the destination and stop with the operator before boarding.

### How do I reach the M1 after arriving at Milano Centrale?

Enter the Metro at Centrale using M2 or M3 and change to M1 at the interchange most appropriate for your destination. Check the current route before travelling.

### What should I do if my flight arrives late?

Check the remaining coach services and onward Metro operating times. If public transport is no longer practical, use the official taxi rank or a reliable pre-booked transfer.

### Can I buy a coach ticket after landing?

Tickets are commonly available through operator websites, apps, ticket offices or airport sales points. Availability and conditions vary, so verify the current options before travelling.

### Can I use a Milan Metro ticket on the airport coach?

No. The airport transfer and the Milan urban transport network use different tickets. Purchase the appropriate ticket for each part of the journey.
