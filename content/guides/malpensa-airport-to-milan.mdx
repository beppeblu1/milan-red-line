{/* rx:introduction */}

Getting from **Malpensa Airport to Milan** is straightforward once you make two decisions: which transport option suits your journey and which Milan arrival point is most convenient for your final destination.

The train is usually the most predictable option, coaches are useful for reaching the Milano Centrale area, and an official taxi or private transfer provides door-to-door travel. The best choice depends on your terminal, luggage, group size, arrival time and onward connection.

{/* rx:audience */}

## Who is this guide for?

This guide is useful if you are:

- arriving at Malpensa for the first time;
- unsure whether your flight uses Terminal 1 or Terminal 2;
- comparing the Malpensa Express, an airport coach and a taxi;
- deciding between Milano Centrale and Milano Cadorna;
- continuing towards the Milan Metro or another train;
- travelling with children, heavy luggage or reduced mobility;
- arriving late and concerned about the final connection.

## Malpensa Airport to Milan at a glance

For most visitors, the practical options are:

- **Malpensa Express:** usually the most predictable choice and available from both airport terminals;
- **airport coach:** useful when you want to reach the Milano Centrale area without taking the train;
- **official taxi or private transfer:** useful for door-to-door travel, groups, heavy luggage or difficult arrival times;
- **rental car:** normally unnecessary for a stay focused on Milan, but potentially useful if you are continuing elsewhere.

The most important decision is not simply **train or coach**.

It is:

> Where do you need to go after reaching Milan?

Your answer determines whether Centrale, Cadorna or door-to-door transport is the more practical choice.

{/* rx:area-selection */}

## Start by checking your terminal

Malpensa has two passenger terminals:

- **Terminal 1**
- **Terminal 2**

Before planning the transfer, check the terminal shown by your airline or flight information.

Both terminals have railway stations, but they are separate stops. Trains normally serve Terminal 2 and Terminal 1 before continuing towards Milan, so make sure you follow signs for the station belonging to your terminal.

Do not automatically travel between terminals before checking your departure point. Your train or coach may already serve the terminal where you land.

### After baggage reclaim

Allow enough time to:

1. leave the aircraft;
2. complete passport control when required;
3. collect checked luggage;
4. follow signs for trains, coaches or taxis;
5. buy or activate the correct ticket;
6. reach the departure point.

A scheduled landing time is not the same as the time you will be ready to leave the airport.

## The main ways to reach Milan

### Malpensa Express

The **Malpensa Express** is usually the most predictable public transport option because it is not affected by road traffic.

Railway stations serve both Terminal 1 and Terminal 2, and trains connect the airport with several Milan stations.

The two routes most visitors compare are:

- **Milano Centrale**
- **Milano Cadorna**

Before boarding, check the destination displayed on:

- departure boards;
- platform screens;
- the train itself;
- the official journey planner.

Do not assume that the first Malpensa Express departure is the best one for your journey.

### Why the train works well

The train is particularly useful when you want:

- a journey less affected by traffic;
- a clear connection to Milan's rail or Metro network;
- space to travel with ordinary luggage;
- a predictable arrival station.

Keep your luggage with you and allow time to reach the platform, especially after a long-haul arrival or when travelling with children.

The airport railway ticket is separate from an ordinary Milan urban transport ticket. For the city network after you arrive, see [Milan Public Transport Tickets](/guides/milan-public-transport-tickets).

### Airport coach

Airport coaches generally connect Malpensa with the **Milano Centrale area**.

They may be convenient if:

- Centrale is already your preferred arrival point;
- the next coach leaves considerably sooner than the next suitable train;
- you prefer a direct road connection without navigating the railway station;
- your onward journey begins near Centrale.

Before boarding, verify:

- the operator;
- the destination;
- the terminal stop;
- the departure bay;
- the current timetable.

Coach journey times are more exposed to traffic conditions. Compare the realistic door-to-door journey rather than only the advertised travel time.

Avoid choosing a coach solely because it is the first vehicle you see outside the terminal.

### Official taxi or private transfer

A taxi or transfer offers the simplest door-to-door journey.

It may be worth considering if you are:

- travelling in a group;
- carrying heavy or numerous bags;
- travelling with small children;
- arriving after convenient public transport connections;
- reaching an address that requires several changes;
- travelling with mobility needs.

Use the official taxi rank and avoid accepting unsolicited transport offers inside the terminal.

For a group, compare the total taxi or transfer cost with the combined cost and inconvenience of several public transport tickets. The cheapest option per person is not always the simplest journey overall.

### Rental car

A rental car is normally unnecessary when Milan is your main destination.

Parking, restricted traffic areas and urban congestion can make driving less convenient than rail and Metro travel.

A car may make more sense if you are continuing directly towards a destination poorly served by public transport rather than staying in Milan.

## Milano Centrale or Milano Cadorna?

This is the most important choice for many train passengers.

Neither station is universally better.

Choose according to the journey you need to make after leaving the Malpensa Express.

### Choose Milano Centrale when…

Milano Centrale is often more practical when:

- you are continuing by long-distance or regional train;
- your accommodation is near Centrale;
- your next Metro journey is more convenient from M2 or M3;
- you need to use Centrale as your next transport hub;
- an airport coach is your preferred option.

Centrale is a large, busy station. Allow time to understand where you are going after leaving the airport train.

Our guide to [How to Use Milano Centrale Station](/guides/milano-centrale-station-guide) explains how to navigate the station, find the Metro and continue your journey.

### Choose Milano Cadorna when…

Milano Cadorna is often more practical when:

- your destination is directly served by the **M1 Red Line**;
- your destination is convenient from M2;
- you are heading towards Duomo or the western and north-eastern sections of M1;
- you want a simpler continuation towards Sesto San Giovanni on M1;
- Cadorna reduces the number of changes required.

Cadorna provides direct access to **M1 and M2**.

This can make it especially useful when your final destination lies on the M1 Red Line.

### Do not choose by station name alone

Milano Centrale may sound like the obvious city-centre choice, but its name does not mean that it is best for every destination.

Cadorna can be more convenient for some central locations and for direct access to M1.

Centrale remains more useful for many onward rail journeys.

Use your complete journey—not the perceived importance of the station—to decide.

## How to continue towards Sesto San Giovanni

For travellers continuing towards Sesto San Giovanni, **Cadorna can provide the simpler Metro connection** because M1 is available directly at the station.

From Cadorna:

1. follow signs for the **M1 Red Line**;
2. choose the direction towards the northern terminus serving Sesto;
3. leave at the station closest to your final destination.

From Milano Centrale, you need to enter the Metro network and normally make an interchange before joining M1.

The best route still depends on:

- the exact address;
- current Metro service;
- luggage;
- accessibility needs;
- the time of arrival.

Our guide to [How to Use the Milan Metro](/guides/how-to-use-the-milan-metro) explains directions, termini and interchanges. You can also compare accommodation connections in [Where to Stay Near the M1 Red Line](/guides/where-to-stay-near-the-m1-red-line).

## Which option should you choose?

### Choose the train when you want predictability

The Malpensa Express is usually the first option to consider when:

- you prefer a journey unaffected by road traffic;
- Centrale or Cadorna works well for your destination;
- you are comfortable continuing by Metro, train or taxi;
- your arrival falls within current service hours.

### Choose a coach when Centrale is convenient

An airport coach can be practical when:

- you want to arrive near Milano Centrale;
- the departure time suits you better;
- traffic conditions are acceptable;
- you do not need Cadorna or a direct M1 connection.

### Choose a taxi or transfer for door-to-door simplicity

An official taxi or booked transfer may be preferable when:

- several people are sharing the cost;
- you have difficult luggage;
- public transport requires multiple changes;
- you arrive very late;
- comfort and simplicity matter more than the lowest price.

### Consider the complete journey

The best airport transfer is the one that makes the entire journey easier.

A slightly longer airport train may still be better if it eliminates an interchange.

A coach may appear direct but become slower in heavy traffic.

A taxi may cost more but represent good value for a larger group travelling to one address.

## Arriving late or during disruption

Late arrivals require more planning because public transport options become less frequent.

Before travelling, check:

- the current final train or coach departure;
- your terminal;
- live service updates;
- the time needed for passport control and baggage reclaim;
- whether your onward Metro connection will still be operating.

Do not plan a tight connection using the aircraft's scheduled landing time.

Flights can arrive late, baggage delivery can take longer than expected and airport platforms may require additional walking.

If rail service is unavailable or disrupted, alternatives may include:

- an airport coach still operating at that time;
- an official taxi;
- a pre-booked transfer;
- waiting for the next available service when appropriate.

Operational conditions can change. Verify current information through official airport and transport sources before travelling.

## Travelling with children, luggage or reduced mobility

### Children and family groups

Families often benefit from choosing the option with the fewest changes.

A direct train followed by one Metro journey may be easier than a theoretically faster route involving several interchanges.

Keep additional time for:

- toilets;
- lifts;
- ticket purchase;
- moving between the terminal and station;
- boarding with luggage or a pushchair.

### Heavy luggage

The train remains practical with normal airline luggage, but the onward journey matters.

Consider:

- station lifts and escalators;
- Metro interchanges;
- the final walk;
- stairs at your destination;
- whether a taxi from Centrale or Cadorna would simplify the last part.

### Reduced mobility

Do not assume that every stage of the journey will be step-free.

Before travelling, verify:

- airport assistance arrangements;
- station accessibility;
- lift availability;
- the accessibility of required Metro stations;
- whether an official taxi or transfer offers a more reliable solution.

Accessibility equipment can occasionally be unavailable, so check current information for the stations included in your route.

## Common mistakes to avoid

### Boarding the wrong Malpensa Express

Check whether the train is going to **Centrale** or **Cadorna**.

The service name alone does not identify the Milan destination.

### Ignoring the airport terminal

Confirm whether your flight arrives at Terminal 1 or Terminal 2 before following transfer instructions.

### Choosing Centrale automatically

Centrale is not always the best arrival station.

Cadorna may be easier for M1 and some central or western destinations.

### Using the wrong ticket

A standard Milan urban ticket is not the airport railway ticket.

Treat the airport transfer and your onward urban journey as separate ticketing decisions.

### Comparing only advertised journey times

Include:

- waiting time;
- walking;
- baggage reclaim;
- interchanges;
- traffic;
- the final journey to your address.

### Leaving no margin after landing

The time between touchdown and reaching the railway platform can vary considerably.

Avoid booking plans that depend on an unrealistically tight airport connection.

## Local tip

> Choose your Milan arrival station according to the Metro line or train you need next—not according to which station name sounds more important.

For travellers continuing on **M1**, Cadorna may require fewer steps. For onward national or regional rail travel, Centrale is often more practical.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does the Malpensa Express leave from both terminals?

Yes. Malpensa has railway stations serving Terminal 1 and Terminal 2. Check the departure board and follow signs for the station at your arrival terminal.

### Should I take the train to Milano Centrale or Milano Cadorna?

Choose Centrale when you need onward rail travel, accommodation near the station or convenient access to M2 or M3. Choose Cadorna when direct access to M1 or M2 creates an easier onward journey.

### Is the train or coach better from Malpensa?

The train is generally more predictable because it avoids road traffic. A coach can be convenient when the Centrale area is your preferred destination and its departure time works better for you.

### How do I reach the M1 after arriving from Malpensa?

A Cadorna-bound train provides direct access to M1 at Milano Cadorna. From Centrale, enter the Metro network and follow the route appropriate for your destination, normally involving an interchange before joining M1.

### What should I do if my flight arrives after the last train?

Check current coach services and the official taxi rank, or arrange a transfer. Always verify the latest operating information before travelling because service times can change.

### Can I buy a Malpensa Express ticket after landing?

Tickets are normally available through official digital and station sales channels. Confirm the correct terminal, destination and departure before purchasing or activating the ticket.

### Is a standard Milan Metro ticket valid on the Malpensa Express?

No. The airport rail journey requires the appropriate railway ticket. Purchase your Milan urban transport ticket separately when continuing on the Metro, tram or bus network.

### How much extra time should I allow with checked luggage?

There is no single reliable allowance because passport control, baggage delivery and walking time vary. Avoid planning around a tight departure and check the next available connections before travelling.

### Good to know

Before leaving Malpensa:

- confirm whether you are at Terminal 1 or Terminal 2;
- check whether your train terminates at Centrale or Cadorna;
- use the correct airport railway ticket rather than a standard urban ticket;
- review current departures and service changes;
- allow additional time after passport control and baggage reclaim;
- choose the connection that works best for your final destination, not simply the first one available.

Timetables, transport operators, accessibility and service conditions can change. Check current information through the official airport, Malpensa Express, Trenord and ATM channels before travelling.
