## Why not stay next to Monza Circuit?

The **Autodromo Nazionale Monza** hosts internationally renowned events throughout the year, attracting large numbers of visitors.

During the Formula 1 Italian Grand Prix and other major weekends, accommodation close to the circuit often sells out early and prices can increase significantly.

Many visitors therefore choose nearby residential areas that offer a wider choice of accommodation while remaining easy to reach by public transport.

When choosing where to stay, think about your entire day: travelling to the circuit, returning after the event and how comfortable the area will be during your stay.

## Getting to Monza Circuit

During most major events, public transport is often the simplest way to reach the circuit, helping visitors avoid traffic and parking restrictions.

Many travellers staying in **Sesto San Giovanni** reach Monza by regional train or through public transport connections, depending on the event and the official travel arrangements.

Before travelling, always check the latest information from the event organisers, as shuttle services and recommended routes may vary.

## Why consider Sesto San Giovanni?

Sesto San Giovanni is one example of an area that offers many of the advantages race weekend visitors look for.

It combines reliable metro and regional rail connections with a quieter residential environment, making it a practical base before and after a busy day at the circuit.

## Local tip

> Plan for extra travel time on major event days.

Large crowds can affect both road traffic and public transport, particularly during the Formula 1 Italian Grand Prix. Leaving a little earlier than planned usually makes the day much more relaxed.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is it necessary to stay near Monza Circuit?

Not necessarily. During major events, many visitors choose well-connected areas outside Monza because they often offer more accommodation options and an easier overall experience.

### How easy is it to reach Monza Circuit by public transport?

During major events, trains and other public transport services are usually the most practical option. Always follow the official travel advice published for your event.

### Is Sesto San Giovanni a practical base for race weekends?

For many visitors, yes. It combines reliable transport, useful everyday services and a quieter residential environment while remaining well connected to both Monza and Milan.

### What should I consider during major events?

Accommodation often sells out early and transport services can become very busy. Booking in advance and planning your journey before travelling can make your visit much smoother.

### Is an apartment a good choice for a race weekend?

If you're staying several nights or travelling with family or friends, an apartment often provides more space, flexibility and comfort than a standard hotel room.
