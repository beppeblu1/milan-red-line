Whether you're visiting the **University of Milano-Bicocca** for a conference, graduation, research project, open day or to see family and friends, choosing the right accommodation can make your visit much easier.

Many people assume they should stay as close to the campus as possible. In reality, accommodation with an easy daily journey and reliable public transport often offers greater flexibility throughout your stay.

## Why not stay next to the university?

The University of Milano-Bicocca attracts students, researchers, conference attendees and international visitors throughout the year.

Because the campus is spread across several buildings and is well served by public transport, many visitors choose accommodation outside the immediate university area without sacrificing convenience.

When deciding where to stay, think about how easily you'll reach the buildings you'll actually visit, how comfortable the neighbourhood will be after your daily activities and whether you'll also spend time exploring Milan.

## Getting to Bicocca University

The university is easy to reach using Milan's metro, railway and bus network.

Typical journey times are around **20–30 minutes from Sesto San Giovanni**, **20 minutes from Duomo** and **15–20 minutes from Milano Centrale**.

Actual journey times depend on the faculty or university building you are visiting and on current public transport services.

## Why consider Sesto San Giovanni?

Sesto San Giovanni is one example of an area that combines many of the qualities university visitors look for.

It offers direct metro and regional rail connections together with a quieter residential atmosphere, making it particularly suitable for visitors staying several days.

If you're unfamiliar with the area, our guide to [whether Sesto San Giovanni is a good place to stay](/guides/is-sesto-san-giovanni-a-good-place-to-stay) explains why many travellers choose it as a base.

Alongside convenient transport, you'll find supermarkets, cafés, restaurants, pharmacies and other everyday services within walking distance.

## More than the university

If you're staying in Milan for several days, good transport connections make it easy to explore the city once your university activities are over.

From Sesto San Giovanni you can easily reach central Milan, while the nearby railway station also provides convenient connections towards **Monza**, **Lecco** and destinations around **Lake Como**.

If you'd like to explore further, our guide to [places to visit near Sesto San Giovanni](/guides/places-to-visit-near-sesto-san-giovanni) offers ideas for discovering North Milan and the surrounding area.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is it better to stay next to Bicocca University?

Not necessarily. Thanks to Milan's public transport network, many well-connected neighbourhoods provide an easy journey to the campus while offering a wider choice of accommodation.

### How long does it take to reach Bicocca University?

Journey times depend on where you're staying and which university building you're visiting, but the campus is well connected by metro, rail and bus.

### Is Sesto San Giovanni convenient for university visitors?

For many visitors, yes. It offers reliable transport, useful everyday services and a quieter residential environment while remaining well connected to Bicocca University.

### Can I explore Milan after university activities?

Yes. Choosing accommodation with good transport connections makes it easy to visit central Milan, restaurants, museums and other attractions once your day at the university is over.

### Is an apartment suitable for longer university visits?

If you're staying for several days, travelling with family or attending conferences, an apartment often provides more space and flexibility than a hotel.
